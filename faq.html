VELCYRO / HELP

FAQ.

What is VELCYRO?

VELCYRO is a fashion concept focused on graphic, anime-inspired and contemporary streetwear aesthetics.

Are the products real?

This current storefront is a demo. Product data and checkout behavior are placeholders until real products and services are connected.

Can I place an actual order?

No. The current GitHub version uses a simulated checkout and browser-local order history.

How will shipping work?

The frontend currently contains a configurable demo shipping calculation. A live store should connect it to your actual shipping policy and fulfillment system.

How do I contact VELCYRO?

Use the contact page once your final support email and business details are ready.
